Phillips Spectrum Ocean

After Jerry Tessendorf, “Simulating Ocean Water” — a Phillips spectrum, evolved by the deep-water dispersion relation and inverted with an FFT on the GPU.

 

Parameters
WebGL2 with float render targets is required

This page runs the FFT on the GPU, which needs WebGL2 and the EXT_color_buffer_float extension. Safari 15+, Chrome and Firefox all support it.
